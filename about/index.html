About Red Ruby Cleaning Co.

Cambridge locals cleaning Cambridge properties. Simple as that.

Our Story

Red Ruby Cleaning Co. was founded right here in Cambridge by a local single mum who saw a gap in the market: Cambridge Airbnb hosts and short-term let owners were desperate for reliable, professional cleaning they could actually count on.

What started with one property and a lot of elbow grease has grown into Cambridge's most trusted Airbnb cleaning service. But we've never lost sight of what matters — turning up on time, doing a brilliant job, and treating every Cambridge property like it's our own.

We're not backed by some faceless corporation. We're a proper British business, registered in England, paying our taxes in the UK, and putting money back into the Cambridge community. Every penny you spend with Red Ruby stays local.

With decades of combined experience in both domestic and commercial cleaning, our team knows what "clean" really means. And we've got the five-star reviews from Cambridge hosts to prove it. We don't do shortcuts — unless it's a faster route through Cambridge traffic.
