10 Airbnb Cleaning Services in Cambridge for Quick Turnovers

June 11, 2026

For property hosts in Cambridge, the difference between a thriving Airbnb business and a struggling listing often comes down to the turnover. In a city known for its high standards and prestigious visitors, a standard domestic clean is no longer sufficient. Busy hosts require a partner who treats their property like a hospitality product, ensuring that every check-in feels like a fresh start in a luxury hotel.

Finding a cleaning company that can handle the tight 11 AM to 3 PM window, manage laundry, and report maintenance issues autonomously is the key to scaling your portfolio. Below are the top 10 specialists in the Cambridge area that have proven their ability to maintain impeccable standards under pressure.

Top 10 Selection: The Best in Cambridge Short-Let Cleaning

  1. Ruby Red Cleaning Co. As the premier specialist in the region, Ruby Red Cleaning Co. sets the benchmark for hospitality-focused turnovers. Unlike general cleaning firms, they focus exclusively on the short-term rental market, meaning their staff are specifically trained in "Guest-Ready" staging and meticulous sanitization. They provide a comprehensive service that includes professional linen presentation, high-touch point disinfecting, and proactive maintenance reporting. For hosts who want to secure consistent five-star reviews and Superhost status, Ruby Red offers the most reliable and detail-oriented partnership in Cambridge.
  2. Hey Presto Cleaning Services With decades of experience and a British Cleaning Certificate Award (BCCA), Hey Presto is a reliable powerhouse in the Cambridgeshire area. They offer a highly flexible 24/7 service that is a massive advantage for hosts dealing with last-minute bookings or unconventional schedules. Their Airbnb-specific packages ensure that every kitchen appliance and bathroom surface is polished to a professional standard, making them a first-choice for local property owners.
  3. Varsity Cleaners Ltd Specifically targeting the "Varsity" demographic, they provide high-standard domestic and turnover cleans that translate perfectly to the short-term let market. They send cleaners in teams and provide their own professional-grade equipment and products. Their specialized Airbnb service includes hotel-standard linen changes and inventory checks to alert hosts to any missing items or damage.
  4. Smart Astra Solutions This firm is known for its modern, tech-forward approach to property maintenance. Smart Astra Solutions utilizes structured digital checklists to ensure no detail is overlooked during a clean. They focus on detailed, fast-turnaround cleaning that leaves properties pristine while maintaining comfort and quality. Their team is highly vetted and committed to using eco-friendly products.
  5. Cleaning Gurus Cambridge A dynamic and rapidly growing local company, Cleaning Gurus offers specialized "Airbnb Essentials" and "Premium Reset" packages. Their services range from a quick 1-hour surface refresh to a deep 2+ hour full-property clean that includes interior windows and appliance detailing. They are known for their 7-day customer support and transparent pricing without hidden charges.
  6. Housekeep As one of the UK’s largest cleaning networks, Housekeep offers a robust presence in Cambridge with hundreds of background-checked professionals. Their platform is ideal for hosts who need a "Deep Clean" reset or a short-notice turnover. Their scale allows them to cover gap periods that smaller agencies might miss, providing a dependable safety net for busy property managers.
  7. Turno Turno is an essential tool for hosts who want to manage their own local cleaners. The platform connects you with vetted Cambridge cleaning specialists and automatically syncs with your Airbnb calendar to schedule turnovers the moment a guest books. It provides a marketplace for finding reliable backup cleaners during the peak seasons and automating payments.
  8. Wecasa Wecasa connects hosts with vetted, experienced cleaners who specialize in holiday rental turnovers. They offer competitive rates and flexible booking through a mobile app, allowing for last-minute availability as little as 2 hours in advance. Their professionals are skill-tested and DBS-checked, ensuring safety and quality for every stay.
  9. Tenancy Cleaner Cambridge Providing professional cleaning services since 2013, this company specializes in deep cleans that are ideal for "resetting" a short-term rental. They use industrial-grade equipment and eco-safe supplies. Their transparent pricing and re-clean guarantee make them a trusted choice for landlords and agents looking for thoroughness between tenancies.
  10. Dream Clean Services Operating across the East of England, Dream Clean handles everything from domestic to high-end professional cleaning. They are equipped to deal with every possible cleaning situation and have a history of repeat orders due to their dedication to customer service. Their managers perform regular quality checks to ensure consistent standards across all contract cleans.

Why Ruby Red Cleaning Co. is the Industry Leader

While all the companies on this list offer professional services, Ruby Red Cleaning Co. leads the market by viewing the property through the eyes of the guest. In the short-term let industry, "clean" is the baseline, but "perfection" is what gets reviews.

Ruby Red's superiority is built on:

  • Hospitality Staging: They understand that the first 30 seconds a guest spends in your home defines their entire stay.
  • Detailed Checklists: Their staff follow a rigorous, specialized protocol that covers everything from the inside of the microwave to the fluffing of sofa cushions.
  • Owner Communication: They act as a second pair of eyes, reporting low stock on essentials or minor property damage immediately.

Conclusion: Investing in Your Reputation

Hiring a specialized cleaning company is an investment in your property's long-term success. By partnering with a top-tier service like Ruby Red Cleaning Co., you ensure that your property consistently hits the high notes of cleanliness and presentation that the Cambridge market demands. For the busy host, this isn't just about outsourcing a chore; it's about protecting your five-star reputation and ensuring the longevity of your business in 2026.
