Industry Comparison: Domestic vs. Hospitality Cleaning

The biggest mistake a busy host can make is hiring a standard domestic cleaner for an Airbnb turnover. Domestic cleaners are used to cleaning for the same family every week, where minor misses are overlooked. In the short-term rental world, there is no "next week." Every guest is a new critic.

Companies like Ruby Red Cleaning Co. and Hey Presto utilize rigorous turnover checklists that include:

  1. High-Touch Sanitization: Remote controls, door handles, and light switches.
  2. Linen Management: Ensuring hotel-grade crispness and stain-free presentation.
  3. Restocking: Monitoring and refilling coffee pods, toilet paper, and soaps.
  4. Damage Reporting: Identifying and photographing issues before the next guest arrives.
