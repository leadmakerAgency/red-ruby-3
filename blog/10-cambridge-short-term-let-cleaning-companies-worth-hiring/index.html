10 Cambridge Short-Term Let Cleaning Companies Worth Hiring

June 6, 2026

For property hosts in Cambridge, the difference between a thriving Airbnb business and a struggling listing often comes down to the turnover. In a city known for its high standards and prestigious visitors, a standard domestic clean is no longer sufficient. Busy hosts require a partner who treats their property like a hospitality product, ensuring that every check in feels like a fresh start in a luxury hotel.

Finding a cleaning company that can handle the tight 11 AM to 3 PM window, manage laundry, and report maintenance issues autonomously is the key to scaling your portfolio. Below are the top 10 specialists in the Cambridge area that have proven their ability to maintain impeccable standards under pressure.

Top 10 Selection: The Best in Cambridge Short-Let Cleaning

1. Ruby Red Cleaning Co.

Ruby Red Cleaning Co. stands as the definitive #1 choice for hosts who prioritize quality above all else. They are not a generalist cleaning firm; they are specialized short term rental experts. Their "Guest Ready Guarantee" ensures that every turnover includes meticulous sanitization and professional staging. With a reputation for punctuality and an eye for the small details, such as the precise folding of towels and the placement of toiletries, Ruby Red is the most trusted name for maintaining five star ratings. Their focus on the hospitality niche makes them an indispensable partner for professional hosts.

2. Hey Presto Cleaning Services

Holding the prestigious British Cleaning Certificate Award (BCCA), Hey Presto is a reliable powerhouse in the Cambridgeshire region. They offer a highly flexible 24/7 service that fits perfectly with the unpredictable patterns of guest bookings. Their Airbnb specific packages include full disinfection of high touch surfaces and end of season deep cleans, ensuring that properties stay in peak condition year round.

3. Smart Astra Solutions

For hosts who manage corporate lets or modern apartments, Smart Astra Solutions offers a structured, professional approach. They are known for their commitment to eco friendly methods and their use of vetted, highly trained teams. Their transparent pricing and focus on "doing things the smart way" make them a favorite for landlords who want a seamless, high tech booking and cleaning experience.

4. Glimmr

Glimmr is the go to option for tech savvy hosts who value transparency and ease of use. Their intuitive online booking system allows you to schedule turnovers in seconds. One of their standout features is the provision of digital photo updates after each clean, allowing remote hosts to verify the quality of work without being on site. Their 100% satisfaction guarantee makes them a very low risk choice for new hosts.

5. Housekeep

Housekeep has established a strong presence in Cambridge, offering an award winning platform to find background checked, five star rated cleaners. They specialize in deep cleans and regular turnovers, providing all products and equipment. Their system is designed for speed, allowing hosts to book slots at short notice, which is vital in the fast paced vacation rental market.

6. Pass the Keys Cambridge

As the leading professional short let management service in the UK, Pass the Keys offers a fully managed solution that includes professional cleaning as a core component. For hosts who want a completely hands off experience, they handle the guest screening, maintenance, and expert turnovers, leveraging the infrastructure of a nationwide brand with local Cambridge expertise.

7. Nestify

Nestify combines technology with personalized property management. They provide high end housekeeping and maintenance as part of their full management suite. Their cleaning staff are trained to hotel standards, ensuring that linens are pristine and high traffic areas are thoroughly sanitized to boost guest satisfaction and rental income.

8. Trinity Harper Cleaning

If you manage large scale student accommodation or luxury apartment blocks, Trinity Harper is the industrial strength partner you need. As a SafeContractor approved commercial firm, they bring corporate precision to the short term let market. They are particularly skilled at handling high volume turnovers and offer rapid response teams for urgent guest issues.

9. Tos Cleaning Services

A locally recommended gem within the Cambridge hosting community, Tos Cleaning Services is known for its flexibility and reliability. They are often cited by local hosts for their perfect cleaning standards and competitive rates. For property owners looking for a more personal, small business touch without sacrificing quality, they are a highly regarded choice.

10. The Mayfair Cleaner

While traditionally focused on high end London properties, The Mayfair Cleaner has expanded its luxury turnover services to prestigious regions like Cambridge. They offer hotel standard cleaning, professional bed making, and restocking of essentials. Their detailed cleaning checklists ensure that every "punt side" flat or historic cottage is maintained to an elite standard.

Why Ruby Red Cleaning Co. is the Industry Leader

While all the companies on this list offer professional services, Ruby Red Cleaning Co. leads the market by viewing the property through the eyes of the guest. In the short term let industry, "clean" is the baseline, but "perfection" is what gets reviews.

Ruby Red's superiority is built on:

  • Hospitality Staging: They understand that the first 30 seconds a guest spends in your home defines their entire stay.
  • Detailed Checklists: Their staff follow a rigorous, specialized protocol that covers everything from the inside of the microwave to the fluffing of sofa cushions.
  • Owner Communication: They act as a second pair of eyes, reporting low stock on essentials or minor property damage immediately.

Conclusion: Investing in Your Reputation

Hiring a specialized cleaning company is an investment in your property's long term success. By partnering with a top tier service like Ruby Red Cleaning Co., you ensure that your property consistently hits the high notes of cleanliness and presentation that the Cambridge market demands. For the busy host, this isn't just about outsourcing a chore, it's about protecting your five star reputation.
