Running an Airbnb in Cambridge means quick turnarounds, high expectations, and **no time for guesswork** between guests.

This turnover checklist is built around what we see every day in local properties — from compact city-centre flats to family homes just outside the ring road.

## Before you start

- Open all curtains and blinds to **let natural light in** — it shows up dust and streaks.
- Put on fresh gloves and gather all products, bin liners, and clean microfibre cloths in a caddy.
- Turn off any loud music so you can **hear maintenance issues** like dripping taps or noisy fans.

## Kitchen

- Empty the fridge of guest food (unless agreed otherwise) and **wipe all shelves and door seals**.
- Check every cupboard and drawer for crumbs, spills, or forgotten items.
- Clean the hob, oven front, splashback, and **handles guests touch constantly**.
- Run the dishwasher empty with a cleaner if it smells musty.

## Bathroom

- Descale taps, shower heads, and glass screens — Cambridge water is *hard* and limescale builds fast.
- Scrub grout lines, especially around the shower and floor edges.
- Replace all towels with freshly laundered sets, folded consistently every time.

## Final five-minute sparkle

- Fluff cushions, straighten rugs, and **align dining chairs**.
- Do a slow walk-through from the front door, exactly as a guest would.
- Finish with a quick lint-roll of sofas and fabric headboards.

With a reliable turnover checklist, you can confidently hand cleaning over to a professional team — and still know every stay starts guest-perfect.+
