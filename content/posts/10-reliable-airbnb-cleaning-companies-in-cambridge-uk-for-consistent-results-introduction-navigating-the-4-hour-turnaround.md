## **The 2026 Cambridge Standard: Why Specialization Matters**

In the demanding Cambridge hospitality market, a standard domestic clean is no longer enough to protect your Superhost status or maintain top placement on booking platforms. International academics, tech consultants, and luxury tourists expect professional, hotel-grade precision.

### **Critical Factors to Consider:**

- **Hard Water Remediation:** Cambridge water is notoriously hard. Specialized firms like **Hey Presto** and **Cleaning Gurus** build systematic limescale descaling into their standard bathroom protocols to keep fixtures gleaming.
- **Proactive Property Auditing:** Reliable partners do not just mop; they check that the Wi-Fi router is plugged in, test the light bulbs, and report property damage immediately following guest checkout.
- **Sanitization Verification:** High-touch areas like keyboxes, remote controls, door handles, and appliances are treated with antibacterial products as a standard step, reassuring health-conscious modern travelers.
