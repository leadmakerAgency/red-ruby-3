## What End-of-Tenancy Experts Actually Do

Turnover cleaning is not the same as a regular domestic spruce. It is a systematic top-to-bottom reset intended to satisfy an inventory clerk. High-quality teams work in zones and stages.

### Kitchen: Degrease, Detail, and Deodorise

The kitchen tends to decide a deposit. Ovens are dismantled where possible, with racks, trays, rails, and glass panels cleaned separately. Extractor hoods are degreased and filters either cleaned or replaced if agreed. Sinks are descaled, drains flushed, and the underside of taps polished. Cupboards are emptied, vacuumed, and wiped inside and out, including the top edge lip that often collects grease.

Appliances matter. Fridges and freezers are defrosted, drip trays cleaned, seals scrubbed, and any lingering odours neutralised. Washing machine drawers and rubber seals are cleaned, and the filter checked. Dishwashers get their filters emptied and spray arms wiped. Behind and under appliances is cleared where access is safe. Tiles and splashbacks are degreased until they pass the fingertip test.

### Bathrooms: Limescale and Detail Work

Cambridge water is mineral rich, so limescale is the enemy. Professional teams use the right descalers, then rinse and buff to a streak-free finish. Shower screens are polished both sides. Grout is scrubbed. Sealant edges are cleaned carefully. Toilets, including the hinge area and the underside of the rim, are sanitised. Extractor fans are dusted. Mirrors and chrome fittings are dried to a shine.

### Living Areas and Bedrooms: Reset to Neutral

End-of-tenancy cleaning aims for neutral, not scented or styled. Dusting includes skirting boards, door frames, tops of doors, light fittings, curtain rails, and reachable cobwebs. Switches, sockets, and handles are degreased. Built-in wardrobes and drawers are wiped inside and out. Radiators are dusted, especially the fins. Window sills and frames are detailed. If furnished, beds are vacuumed and under-bed areas cleared.

### Floors and Carpets: Edges and Traffic Lanes

Floors are vacuumed and mopped, but the key deposit savers are the edges and traffic lanes. Good cleaners use crevice tools along skirting. For carpets, many end-of-tenancy jobs include professional hot water extraction to lift pet hair, odours, and old stains. This is usually an add-on cost and can be a condition with some leases.

### Windows and Extras

Internal glass is cleaned, sills and tracks detailed. Blinds are dusted or wiped slat by slat. Light shades, vents, and smoke alarm covers are dusted. Exterior windows can be arranged if accessible or via a window cleaning partner. Garden tidying, bin cleaning, and garage sweeping are usually extras and should be agreed upfront.

## How to Choose Turnover Cleaners Near Me Cambridge

Typing turnover cleaners near me Cambridge yields dozens of options. The difference between a smooth handover and a fraught re-clean often comes down to a few checks.

Ask about their end-of-tenancy guarantee. Many reputable teams offer a re-clean within 24 to 72 hours if the agent flags specific misses on the checkout report. This is not a promise to fix damage or wear and tear, only to address cleaning issues. Make sure the guarantee is in writing.

Check they are insured and can provide a receipt that references end-of-tenancy cleaning. Agents sometimes ask for proof a professional clean was carried out. Insurance matters in tight Cambridge terraces with tricky parking and narrow staircases.

Ask if they follow or can match your agent’s checklist. If your letting agent is using a standard set, share it in advance. A professional team will map their work to that list and explain any reasonable adjustments.

Look at reviews with intent. Ignore the glossy marketing. Read the three and four star reviews. These often tell you how companies handle problems, timing delays, and re-cleans. Pay attention to mentions of deposit returns, inventory clerk satisfaction, and punctuality.

Discuss access and logistics. Many homes near the station, along Mill Road, and in the city centre have permit parking or tight access. A good company will ask about parking options and lift access. They should also be comfortable with key collection from your agent or a lockbox if needed.

Consider equipment and eco options. Ask whether they bring all materials and tools. If you prefer eco-friendly products, check they can use them while still removing limescale and heavy grease. Some tasks need stronger solutions, but many teams can balance both.

## Pricing in Cambridge: What Affects the Cost

Prices vary with size, condition, and extras. In Cambridge, a professional end-of-tenancy clean for a typical one-bedroom flat often sits between £140 and £220. A two-bedroom might range from £180 to £280, and a three-bedroom from about £240 to £380. Larger homes in Newnham or Trumpington, or houses with extensions and conservatories, go higher. These are guide figures, not quotes.

Add-ons can shift the total. Full oven cleaning can be £40 to £70 depending on type. Carpet cleaning might be £20 to £35 per room, with staircases priced separately. Upholstery, blinds, exterior windows, and patios usually cost extra. Heavy mould treatment or paint scuff removal is often out of scope and classed as maintenance, not cleaning.

For an accurate quote, be ready to share:

- Number of bedrooms and bathrooms, plus any additional rooms like studies or utility rooms
- Flooring types, carpeted areas, and whether you want professional carpet cleaning
- Appliance count and type, especially double ovens or range cookers
- Condition notes such as pets, smoking, strong cooking odours, or heavy limescale
- Access and parking details, including permits or height restrictions
- The date and your checkout schedule

Fixed-price quotes are common for end-of-tenancy work because the team commits to an outcome. Hourly rates can look cheaper, but you want an outcome guarantee rather than a time cap. Confirm VAT status if relevant and always ask for a written quote.

## Timing, Logistics, and Cambridge-Specific Quirks

End-of-month slots book fast. In Cambridge, there is a summer surge when student lets turnover between July and September. If you need a weekend clean or a same-day turnaround before a morning checkout, reserve early.

Schedule the clean when the property is empty, or as empty as possible. Inventory clerks prefer to check an unfurnished or cleared space. If you must overlap moving and cleaning, separate rooms and keep cleaners informed.

Leave utilities on. Professionals need hot water and electricity for vacuums, extraction machines, and steamers. If the power is off, tell the cleaners in advance so they can plan, or better yet keep it on until after checkout.

Plan access. If your place is in CB1 near the station or in the city centre, arrange visitor permits or pay-and-display. In terraced streets off Mill Road or in Chesterton, parking is tight and affects the team’s time. Key pickup from your agent can save you a journey.

Allow time before the checkout. Ideally, book the clean 24 hours before the inventory inspection. This gives you buffer time to air the property and handle any last-minute agent requests.

## Preparing Your Property Before Cleaners Arrive

Good preparation makes a noticeable difference. Remove personal items and rubbish so cleaners can work efficiently. Empty cupboards and drawers. Defrost the freezer the night before. Replace blown lightbulbs if they were working at move-in. Take photos of any pre-existing damage that is not a cleaning issue, like a cracked tile or scratched worktop.

If you have pets, vacuum once before the team arrives. It is not mandatory, but it helps. If blinds are delicate or there are special finishes, point those out. Provide instructions for any locked areas, garden access, or outbuildings.

## Common Pitfalls That Cost Deposits

Most deposit deductions arise from a few repeat issues. Ovens and extractor hoods are the top offenders. Next come limescale on bathroom fixtures, unclean fridge seals, and missed areas like skirting tops and window tracks. Carpets with pet odours or visible stains are another problem, especially if the tenancy agreement called for professional cleaning.

Remember the difference between cleaning and wear. End-of-tenancy cleaners can remove dirt, grease, mould on sealant edges, and dust. They cannot fix scratched floors, dented worktops, peeling paint, or scorched hobs. Those are maintenance or damage items, resolved through repairs or landlord negotiation. A thorough clean reduces the list to genuine wear and tear questions, which helps your case.

## When a Re-clean Is Appropriate

Even excellent teams can miss a spot, particularly in large properties. A good guarantee gives you a fallback. If your agent flags a cleaning issue on the checkout report, share that document immediately. Be specific. For example, if the note says extractor hood greasy under the lip, provide a photo. Professional companies will return within a set window and address those points.

A re-clean is not a redo of everything, and it is not a fix for new mess created after the original job. It targets the clerk’s points and gets you across the line. Keep the property secure and free from re-occupation until the re-check.
