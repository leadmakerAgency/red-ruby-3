When guests say *“clean”*, they rarely mean *“fine”*. They mean **“I couldn’t find a single thing wrong”**.

In Cambridge, where guests are often here for graduations, conferences, or once-in-a-lifetime visits, expectations are sky-high — and so are the stakes for your reviews.

## Focus on the \"review magnets\"

Some areas almost always get mentioned in reviews:

- **Bathroom shine** — taps, glass, tiles, and grout must look freshly done.
- **Kitchen surfaces & appliances** — no crumbs, no greasy fingerprints, no mystery splashes.
- **Bed presentation** — crisp linen, smooth duvets, and fluffed pillows.

## Build a pre-arrival ritual

Before every check-in, run through the same short sequence:

1. Open windows for a few minutes to refresh the air.
2. Double-check toilets, sinks, and bins for any missed marks.
3. Smooth bedding and straighten bedside items.
4. Wipe fingerprints from door handles and light switches.

## When to bring in professionals

If you’re juggling work, family, and hosting, cleaning can quickly become the thing you **rush** instead of **master**.

Many Cambridge hosts now book professional cleaners for:

- Every turnover in peak season.
- Monthly deep cleans to reset the property.
- Specialist tasks like oven or carpet cleaning.

With the right routine — and, when needed, the right help — 5-star cleanliness reviews stop being a pleasant surprise and become your **default**.+
